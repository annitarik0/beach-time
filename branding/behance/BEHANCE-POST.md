**Description:**

Beach Time is a free app that reads weather, water, waves, tides, UV and safety and turns them into one number: a score from 0 to 100 that tells you if today is a beach day, and the best hour to go.

The identity is built from three things you can see from the sand: the sun, the wave and the number. The name rides a wave, the symbol is the sun rising over the swell, and the score itself becomes the hero of every poster, post and screen.

Three modes, three moods: Swim in foam and lagoon blues, Surf in night ink and wax orange, Chill in gold and sunset. A graphic system of tide lines, sunset stripes and score rings carries the brand from the app to towels, totes, beach flags, billboards and a daily social post.

Typefaces: Bricolage Grotesque and Manrope (both open source).
