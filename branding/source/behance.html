01 — The ideaBeach Time

Read the ocean.

Beach Time reads weather, water, waves, tides, UV and safety and turns them into one number: a score from 0 to 100 that tells you if today is a beach day, and the best hour to go.

The identity is built from three things you can see from the sand: the sun, the wave and the number.

02 — SymbolConstruction

The sun comes up over the swell.

A half sun on the horizon at 60% of the square, a foam crest, and a deep-water line at 77%. Corners are 22.5% of the size, the same as an iPhone icon, so it feels at home on a phone.

03 — WordmarkThe tide line

The name rides a wave. Lowercase Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold set on a sine curve, so the letters rise and fall like a swell. A tide line underneath echoes the foam in the symbol.
