01 · The idea02

A friend who has already checked the sea.

Beach Time turns weather, water, waves, tides, UV and safety into one score from 0 to 100, for swimming, surfing or lying in the sun, and tells you the best time to go. The brand should feel like that friend: warm, sure of the facts, never loud.

Warm

Sun-washed paper, soft corners, a voice that sounds like a person.

Exact

Real numbers in every line. 21° water, 0.9 m waves, UV 6.

Safe

Flags, rips and storms come first. Safety is never a joke.

Calm

One clear answer per screen. Motion as slow as a swell.

Give it room. Keep it whole.

08 · Motion09

Move like the tide.

Sunrise

The intro: a 3D sun climbs over a live sea while the score counts to 100. Plays once per visit, about 3 s.

power2.out · 2.8 s
Tide wipe

Three layers of sea wash across the screen and back out to reveal the page. Used for big transitions only.

power4.inOut · 0.9 s · 90 ms stagger
Swell

Idle things float gently, like a phone resting on water. Small travel (16 px, 1.5°), long loops, never jumpy.

sine.inOut · 3.2 s yoyo
